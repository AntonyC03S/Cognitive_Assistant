Tutorial

  1. On the next page, click Generate QR.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone camera.
  3. On your phone, choose an image and tap Upload.
  4. The image will appear on the QR page automatically.
  5. You can view all images in My uploads.
  6. The center timer counts down from 30:00. You can pause it.
  7. When it hits 00:00 (or you click End), you will be logged out and taken to the ending screen.
