Instructions

Using the QR upload

  1. Log in.
  2. Accept consent.
  3. On the QR page, click Generate QR.
  4. Scan the QR code with your phone.
  5. Pick an image and press Upload.
  6. The image appears on the QR page and in My uploads.

Timer

  • The session timer starts at 30:00.
  • Pause freezes it. Resume continues it.
  • End logs you out and takes you to the ending screen.
  • When the timer hits 00:00, it automatically ends the session.

Troubleshooting

  • If your phone can’t open the QR link, you are likely using localhost. Use a tunnel or deploy.
  • If uploads succeed but don’t show, refresh the QR page and confirm you’re logged in.
